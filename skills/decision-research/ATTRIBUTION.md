# Attribution and method influences

This package contains original Codex instructions and templates. It does not copy or vendor code, prompts, model weights or datasets from the works below.

## FS-Researcher

The file-based separation between durable research context and report writing was influenced by:

- Chiwei Zhu et al., *FS-Researcher: Test-Time Scaling for Long-Horizon Research Tasks with File-System-Based Agents*, arXiv:2602.01566 (2026), <https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.01566>
- Reference repository: <https://github.com/Ignoramus0817/FS-Researcher>

This package adds materiality-based stopping, structured claims and contradictions, financial reconciliation dimensions and independent verification.

## WebWeaver

The evidence-linked planning loop was influenced by:

- Zijian Li et al., *WebWeaver: Structuring Web-Scale Evidence with Dynamic Outlines for Open-Ended Deep Research*, arXiv:2509.13312 (2025), <https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.13312>

## DeepVerifier

The explicit rubric and revision loop was influenced by:

- Yuxuan Wan et al., *Inference-Time Scaling of Verification: Self-Evolving Deep Research Agents via Test-Time Rubric-Guided Verification*, arXiv:2601.15808 (2026), <https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15808>
- Reference repository: <https://github.com/yxwan123/DeepVerifier>

All referenced third-party works retain their own copyright and licence terms.
