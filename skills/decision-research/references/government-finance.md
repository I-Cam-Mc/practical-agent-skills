# Government finance

Preserve the money verb:

`proposed -> authorised -> appropriated -> allocated -> awarded -> contracted -> encumbered -> claimed -> paid or reimbursed -> expended -> reverted`

For every material figure record currency, price basis, fiscal period, amount status, fund source, gross or net basis, one-time or ongoing character, programme scope, administering body, target population and source location.

Never:

- sum different statuses into one total;
- sum an umbrella programme with its components;
- add annual flows to a multiyear total without a crosswalk;
- treat transfers as new spending;
- average conflicting figures;
- label an estimate as actual expenditure.

Report a range or unresolved conflict when the evidence cannot support one total.
