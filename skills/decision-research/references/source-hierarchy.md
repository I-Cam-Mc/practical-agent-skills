# Source hierarchy

Use the highest available tier that directly owns the proposition. Authority is claim-specific.

1. Controlling or primary evidence: legislation, regulations, court decisions, audited statements, signed contracts, original datasets and primary empirical studies.
2. Authoritative analysis: independent audit offices, transparent official evaluations and high-quality systematic reviews.
3. Credible secondary reporting: reputable reporting or analysis with named authors and transparent sourcing.
4. Discovery only: aggregators, marketing pages, undated summaries, social posts and unattributed claims.

A programme page can be primary for its current rules but not for independent effectiveness. Repetition of one underlying source is not independent corroboration. Record both publication and event dates when timing matters.
