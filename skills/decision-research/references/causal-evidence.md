# Causal evidence

Classify outcome evidence as:

- `randomised`
- `strong_quasi_experimental`
- `quasi_experimental`
- `associational`
- `descriptive`

Use causal verbs only when a credible counterfactual and the study's diagnostics support them. Associational evidence supports “was associated with”. Descriptive evidence supports wording such as “coincided with”, “participants reported” or “the programme reached”.

Check population, intervention, comparator, outcome, horizon, assignment, analysis unit, baseline, pre-trends, attrition, missing data, measurement, effect size, uncertainty, practical importance, multiple testing, spillovers, concurrent changes, external validity, funding and evaluator independence.

Separate inputs, implementation, reach, intermediate outcomes, final outcomes and causally attributable effects.
